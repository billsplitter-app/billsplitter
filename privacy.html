Bill Splitter
Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Bill Splitter helps a group split a restaurant bill. It is designed to keep your information on your own phone. This page explains what the app does with information and what to be aware of.

Information we collect

None. Bill Splitter has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers that collect or store your information. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.

Information kept on your phone

  • Names, mobile numbers, email addresses and amounts you type in stay on your phone, inside the app.
  • Only the organiser's own name, mobile number and email address are saved, so they are available next time. Everyone else's details are kept only until the app is closed or reset.
  • "Clear all personal details" in the app removes all names, mobile numbers and email addresses, including the organiser's.

Contacts

If you use "Add from Contacts", the app reads only the one contact you choose. It cannot see the rest of your contacts.

Text messages and emails

Payment records are sent from your own Messages or Mail app, like any message you send. You see the message and tap Send yourself. Bill Splitter does not send or keep copies.

QR codes

Each group's QR code carries its payment record inside the code itself. When it is scanned, a simple web page shows the record on the scanning phone only. The record sits after the # sign in the link, which phones never send over the internet, so it is never sent to or stored on any server. The page also removes the record from the browser history straight away.

The web page is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may record basic technical details of visits, such as the visitor's internet address, for security. It does not receive the payment record.

Payments

Any purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. Bill Splitter never sees your card or bank details.

Things to be aware of

  • Anyone who can see or photograph a QR code can read that group's names and amounts, so only show it to the people it is for.
  • A text or email stays on the receiving phone, and the person who gets it can forward it.
  • A screenshot of a record stays on that phone until it is deleted.

Children

Bill Splitter is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed.
