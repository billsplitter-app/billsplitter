Instructions

As the group leader/payer, please enter your first name and the first names of each of the members for whom you will be paying. If your first name is the same as another group leader’s name, please add another letter or character to make your name unique. Tick the drinker box of any group member who was a drinker. All this will help the organiser.

Please enter any special food amounts, the description of each food item, and whether the cost should replace the average food bill or be added to it. For example, if the member was the only person having a dessert, the cost should be added to the average food bill. If they had an entrée in place of a main, the amount should replace the average food bill.

⚠️ Please check your group leader's first name carefully. It must be spelt exactly as they entered it, or you won't be added to their group.
